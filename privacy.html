POV Blaster Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 24, 2026

POV Blaster is a free game. This notice explains the limited information used when you submit a score to the shared leaderboard.

What is collected

When a browser submits a score, the service stores the player name, kill count, submission time, and the city and country inferred from the request IP address. The API sends the request IP transiently to ipapi.co for that lookup; the raw IP address is not stored in the score database.

Why it is used

The player name and kill count operate the shared leaderboard. The approximate city and country provide aggregate context about where players are connecting from. Location may be unavailable or inaccurate.

Storage and retention

Scores are stored by the POV-Blaster API on its hosted database. Local browser storage may retain a fallback copy when the API is unavailable. Scores remain until the project owner removes them or the service is retired.

Sharing

Leaderboard records are publicly retrievable through the game API, including player name, kills, approximate location, and submission time. Do not use a name or other text that identifies you if you do not want it displayed publicly.

Your choices

Submitting a score is optional. You can play without submitting a score, and you can request deletion of a score by contacting the project owner through the project repository.
